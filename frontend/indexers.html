How it works

From upload to report in four steps

Every artifact is hashed, inspected statically and scored before it ever reaches a report.

1

Authenticate

Sign in with an investigator ID. Sessions are scoped so each analyst only sees their own cases.

2

Open a case

Group related evidence under a case with a name and description, then track it to closure.

3

Upload evidence

Drop in a file, paste a URL, or forward a raw email. Nothing leaves the browser.

4

Read the verdict

What we detect

Static analysis built for triage

Heuristics that surface the patterns analysts look for first — without running the sample.

PE

Executables & droppers

PE headers, packer sections, high-entropy segments, injection API chains and download-and-execute behaviour.

URL

Phishing & DGA domains

Punycode homographs, typosquatting, suspicious TLDs, shorteners, credential paths and algorithmic domains.

@

Email spoofing

SPF, DKIM and DMARC failures, Reply-To and Return-Path mismatches, risky attachments and embedded links.

IMG

Steganography

Data appended past JPEG EOI and PNG IEND markers, plus embedded PE, ZIP, PDF and ELF signatures.

IOC

Threat intel matching

Optional URLhaus CSV import for exact URL and hostname matches, on top of the built-in heuristics.

RPT

Findings & reports

Severity-ranked findings with highlighted evidence snippets, rolled into a printable investigation report.

Built for investigators

Reports that explain the score, not just the verdict

Each exported report connects the final risk score to the exact findings that influenced it, with evidence hashes and analyst-facing limitations.

01

Score breakdown

Finding-by-finding points, severity, why each rule fired, and how the aggregate score is capped.

02

Evidence integrity

SHA-256 hashes, upload timestamps, evidence type, and available file metadata for traceability.

03

Investigation context

Case status, evidence inventory, threat-intelligence availability, limitations, and recommended next steps.
